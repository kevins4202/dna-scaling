# Fair six-generator reassessment with the reproduced CNNs

This is a separately registered evaluation. No generator or CNN is retrained. All original generations, natural references, earlier scores and frozen project CNN remain unchanged.

The reproduction produced 30 domain-specific leave-one-tag-out detectors for its initial 32-chunk configuration. We fix a single ensemble before evaluating project outputs: average logits within each domain and give the three domains equal weight. All six generators receive the same 30 weights, not generator-specific model selection. Per-domain ensembles are secondary diagnostics, not candidates from which to select the most favorable ranking.

Use the existing matched 100-prompt panel and saved 2048-base continuations. Exclude the conditioning prefix from CNN inputs. Every detector scores two adjacent 1024-base windows, then logits are averaged over windows and the fixed domain-balanced ensemble. We do not claim these two-window scores replicate the original long-sequence 32-chunk benchmark. FP32 inference, evaluation mode, no augmentation or recalibration; detector checkpoints strictly loaded and checked unchanged.

Before any CNN inference, screen all natural prefix+continuation sequences and all six generated sequences against the complete original CNN benchmark corpus and the known small-language-model training FASTA. This is stricter than screening only a chosen detector's fit/calibration subset. Retain original sequence bytes and source provenance. Use two minimap2 alignment passes (asm10 and sensitive sr k11/w5) and exclude a complete source group across all six generators if any reported alignment spans at least256 query bases at at least90% identity. Exact whole-record matches and reverse complements are also checked. Source rows used by our language models are excluded by coordinates. Every removal and alignment is retained; no prompts are substituted. Exclusions based on any generator apply to all models and are reported as an eligibility restriction, not hidden evidence of better generation.

No detected match at finite sequence-screening thresholds does not prove species or phylogenetic independence. Metadata coverage is incomplete. Released 7B pretraining overlap remains unknown. These qualifications are required; do not label the experiment universally contamination-free.

Register design.json (inputs, detector choice, analysis, screening rule) before screening and protocol.json (eligible IDs and screening evidence) before scoring. Primary score: original category-mixture-weighted within-category AUROC, renormalized over represented categories. Use2000 category-stratified whole-source-group paired bootstrap samples, seed 6220, shared across models and windows. Report all category counts, intervals, all paired aggregate contrasts, per-domain diagnostics, and original CNN scores restricted to exactly the same retained IDs. Intervals are exploratory and unadjusted. One training/generation seed and reused prompts limit interpretation. No automatic scaling-law or biological-function claims.

Code: scripts/reassess_cnn/. Data/screen evidence: data/cnn-reassessment-v1/. Reports: reports/cnn-reassessment-v1/. Queue: scripts/reassess_cnn/queue.sh; physicalGPU7 only with research/distance locks. Completed per-detector scores can be resumed only when bound protocol/checkpoint hashes match. Source/data/checkpoint integrity is checked before and after scoring. Final validation independently checks ensemble weights and point AUROCs with sklearn.

## Verified completion

All 100 prompts from 90 groups passed the registered known-training overlap screen, with no reported qualifying sequence matches and no source-row overlap. All six saved generation sets were scored with the same 30 fixed detectors. Primary aggregate AUROCs are5M 0.5560,10M 0.6030,25M 0.5630,50M 0.5306,100M 0.5080,released 7B 0.5837. Independent ensemble and sklearn metric checks and source/checkpoint integrity passed. The ranking changes relative to our earlier CNN, and secondary score-direction inversions warn of transfer limitations. See reports/cnn-reassessment-v1/SUMMARY.md for intervals, exclusions and interpretation. No retraining was performed.
